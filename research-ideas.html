Droplets, bubbles and interfaces

When does a bubble split around an obstacle?

Study bubble deformation, bypassing, and splitting around a cylinder or fiber, including the intervening liquid film.

Controlling droplets with patterned surfaces

Investigate how surface patterns influence pinning, depinning, and droplet motion.

Droplet impact on a layered liquid pool

Examine how a floating liquid layer changes crater formation, interface deformation, and jet emergence.

What controls the breakup of an immiscible jet?

Study how inertia, viscosity, and surface tension influence breakup length and droplet formation.

How confinement changes rising bubbles

Investigate bubble shape and rise speed in pipes and annular passages.

Moving droplets and bubbles with temperature gradients

Study thermocapillary migration and the influence of confinement and surface-tension response.

How surfactants reshape flow inside droplets

Examine how surfactant transport and thermal stresses interact to change internal circulation.

Can bubble curtains contain a pollutant?

Investigate how bubble injection and curtain arrangement affect pollutant containment and leakage.

Resolving heat transfer around a growing bubble

Assess thermal coupling and interface-resolution effects in a carefully scoped single-bubble problem.

Flow through porous materials

Designing fibrous filters that capture and merge droplets

Examine how fiber arrangement and wettability affect droplet capture, coalescence, and release.

Mobilizing trapped fluids in porous media

Study how pore geometry, wettability, and an injected third fluid influence displacement and trapping.

Which model should we trust?

Which multiphase model should we use—and when?

Compare selected formulations using common benchmarks and quantitative measures of accuracy, conservation, stability, and cost.

Choosing collision kernels for reliable simulations

Investigate how collision models affect numerical dissipation, accuracy, and robustness.

Can interface geometry be calculated locally?

Assess interface gradients and normals reconstructed from population moments, including where corrections are needed.

Combining LBM with a finite-volume interface solver

Compare coupling strategies for consistent mass and momentum transport.

Accurate flows around curved boundaries

Quantify how wall treatments affect forces, near-wall flow, and convergence.

More physics per byte

How much numerical precision does LBM really need?

Assess reduced and mixed precision, round-off mitigation, and the tradeoff between precision, resolution, memory, and runtime.

Faster LBM at the same accuracy

Investigate GPU implementation, memory-efficient schemes, or grid refinement using cost at a specified accuracy.

Unsteady flows and turbulence

Predicting the onset of vortex shedding

Identify wake transitions and instability growth while separating physical behavior from numerical effects.

How reliable are under-resolved turbulent simulations?

Assess how collision and stabilization choices affect dissipation and flow statistics on limited grids.

Choosing a manageable project

Students are expected to provide their own computing hardware. Three-dimensional breakup, thermal multiphase flows, and turbulence can be demanding; the initial test case and resolution will be chosen accordingly.
