A droplet on a vibrating fibre

The purple shape is the liquid droplet; the grey rod is the solid fibre. The fibre is driven to vibrate. Watch how the droplet deforms and eventually detaches.

Heated tubes exchange heat with the surrounding fluid. Watch the rising plumes interact above the two rows of tubes.

04 · Confined flow

A moving lid sets the fluid in motion

A wall-driven flow inside a square cavity. Motion at the lid drives circulation, while smaller structures develop near the walls and within the flow.

05 · Complex flow

The wake behind a sphere

A three-dimensional flow visualization shows intricate structures around and downstream of a sphere. Watch how the wake evolves in the animation.

06 · Shear instability

Kelvin–Helmholtz: from shear to spirals

Two fluid layers move in opposite directions. Small disturbances grow into rolling vortices, which interact, merge and stretch into fine filaments.

07 · Jet into a pool

A jet enters still fluid

Fluid is continuously injected through a narrow opening on the left into an initially quiescent pool. The advancing jet head rolls up, draws in surrounding fluid, and leaves unstable shear layers behind it.

08 · Three-dimensional motion

A vortex ring with a restless core

A ring of rotating fluid carries disturbances around its core. Seeded fluctuations make the ring deform and produce smaller vortical structures around it.
