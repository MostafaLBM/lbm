A first week, if you get hooked

  • Run the cavity at different Reynolds numbers. What changes in the main vortex and the corner eddies?
  • Read the solver on the page. Find the collision and streaming steps.
  • Change one thing: the obstacle size, the lid speed, the collision step.
  • Tell us what you saw. That is a thesis conversation.

What you take away: numerical methods, GPU and HPC skills, verification habits, and scientific C++ or Python that employers and PhD programs value.
