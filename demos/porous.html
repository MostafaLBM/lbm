The core solver



Try it: add rock across a busy throat and watch the flow find another path. Erase rock to open a passage, or generate a new sample. Porosity and grain size rebuild the material; driving force changes the flow through the current sample.


The driving force is a gentle, uniform acceleration to the right. Stationary grains resist the flow through viscous drag. The material repeats at its edges, so fluid leaving one side returns on the opposite side.


D2Q9 lattice Boltzmann with BGK collision, Guo forcing and bounce-back at solid surfaces. Porosity is the fraction of open space; permeability measures how easily the material carries flow. The permeability estimate appears once low-Reynolds-number flow settles.


A two-dimensional model. Narrow pore throats need several lattice cells to resolve; the force is reduced automatically if speeds approach the numerical limit.
