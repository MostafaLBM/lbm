Model assumptions & steam properties

Rankine cycle with boiler pressures from 0.5 to 30 MPa, saturated liquid at the condenser exit, adiabatic pumps and turbines, and no pressure losses. Both turbine stages use the same isentropic efficiency. Reheat returns steam to the maximum temperature. Open heaters mix adiabatically to saturated liquid; closed heaters have zero terminal temperature difference and saturated-liquid drains. Device curves illustrate a constant inlet-referenced isentropic efficiency. Above water’s critical pressure (22.064 MPa), boiler heating is single phase with no boiling plateau; the outlet temperature is kept at or above 374 °C. Feedwater heaters retain subcritical extraction pressures so their saturated-liquid assumptions remain valid.

Water and steam properties use IAPWS-IF97.
