Modifications: only base and forward PT modules are bundled in vendor/if97.js.
The result wrapper returns plain records with pressure in MPa; unused transport
property evaluation and the @neutrium/thermo State dependency are omitted.
The original forward equations and coefficients are retained. model.js adds
explicit two-phase states and numerical inversion of the forward equations
for pressure/enthalpy and pressure/entropy inputs. No endorsement is implied.

Cycle implementation
--------------------
The cycle logic and state-numbering conventions were translated from the
user-supplied rankine_cycle_model.m, rankine_regenerative_model.m and
rankine_state_points.m. The browser interface was rebuilt for this course.

Independent numerical reference
-------------------------------
The original MATLAB application uses XSteam by Magnus Holmgren, www.x-eng.com,
implementing IAPWS-IF97. XSteam is used to generate validation references, not
executed or bundled in the website. Authorship is acknowledged here.

IAPWS-IF97 is the International Association for the Properties of Water and
Steam industrial formulation for water and steam properties.
https://iapws.org/relguide/IF97-Rev.html

Supercritical extension
-----------------------
Boiler pressure extended to 30 MPa. Region-3 density estimates are refined
against the fundamental Helmholtz pressure equation, including stable-branch
selection. In the final 4 kPa below critical pressure, coexistence is resolved
using the two stable branches and matching Gibbs energy to avoid the tiny
region-4/fundamental-EOS mismatch. The vendor bundle additionally exposes the
analytic isothermal pressure-density derivative; coefficients are unchanged.
Pressure/enthalpy and pressure/entropy inversions above critical pressure use a
continuous single-phase branch, with no saturation or vapor-quality calculation.
The critical constants are those of IAPWS-IF97: 22.064 MPa and 647.096 K.
Reference: https://iapws.org/technical-guidance/release/IF97-Rev
